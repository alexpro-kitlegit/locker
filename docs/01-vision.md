# The Locker — vision

Source: [The Locker, a separate company (founders' draft)](source/The%20Locker%20a%20separate%20company.pdf)

**The Locker sells. KitLegit checks.**

The Locker is a marketplace where football fans buy and sell pre-owned shirts. Every shirt is checked as authentic by KitLegit before it goes on sale. It's B2B2C: clubs get their own white-label resale store, and fans trade through it.

## Who's involved

| Party | Role | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| **Fans** (sellers and buyers) | List, buy, get paid | Use The Locker app, or their club's store. Selling is free. |
| **The Locker** (new company) | The marketplace | One app for fans with KitLegit built in, white-label stores for clubs, payments, shipping and support |
| **Clubs** (partners) | Host a store | Their own resale store in club colours on their own web address, 110% credit spent in their shop, and the data |
| **KitLegit** (authentication partner) | Checks every listing | Checks every shirt inside The Locker app and issues the certificate. The Locker pays per check. |
| **FanNFC** (physical tags) | Tamper-proof tags | NFC tags on high-value shirts. Tap to prove it's the shirt we checked. The tag links to the certificate. |
| **Back Four** (brand protection) | Acts on fakes | Fakes are passed on to Back Four, which acts for clubs and brands and opens doors to clubs. |

## One sale, in three steps

1. **List (seller):** add photos and a price in The Locker app. KitLegit checks the shirt there, so a fake is never listed.
2. **Buy (buyer):** buy in the club's store or The Locker app, at one all-in price. We hold the money until the shirt arrives.
3. **Get paid (seller):** choose 110% club credit, spending the money in The Locker, or cash to their bank.

**One app, one store per club.** Fans *sell* in The Locker app. They *buy* there or in their club's store.

### Payout example: a sale of £56.41

| Option | Seller gets | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Club credit (best value) | £62.05 | 110%, spent in the club's shop |
| Spend in The Locker | £56.41 | 100%, used on their next shirt |
| Cash to bank | £53.09 | 5% + 50p fee |

## Surfaces

- **The Locker app (mobile):** not white-labelled. Tabs are Home, Search, Sell, Wallet and Me. "Powered by KitLegit" is shown throughout.
- **The Locker central web app:** the same marketplace on the web.
- **Club stores (white-label):** one site that changes with the club's web address, e.g. `locker.yourclub.com`. The club picks a template and colours. It can be embedded in the club's site or app (as a webview). Buy-only.
- **Club portal:** onboarding, branding, credit settings, analytics, and monthly statements. *(Inferred, not in the PDF.)*
- **Locker ops admin:** disputes, flagged chats, payouts, clubs, and KitLegit checks. *(Inferred: "a person decides every dispute".)*

## Trust and safety

- Money is held until delivery.
- Strict limits against abuse.
- A person decides every dispute.
- AI flags scam messages in chats (phone numbers, bank details, "pay me outside the app").
- ID is only requested when it matters (high-value listings or flagged accounts).

## Business model (per sale, estimates)

- Fees are charged to the buyer in the all-in price. Selling is free.
- Costs: payment and payout fees; **3 KitLegit checks per sale at 14p** (2 listing checks, because half of listings sell, plus 1 dispatch check); fixed costs.
- Profit is about £1.50–2.30 per sale. From that, 50p goes to a support pot and 1% to a buyer-protection pot.
- Clubs and KitLegit are paid once a month by bank transfer, outside Stripe.
